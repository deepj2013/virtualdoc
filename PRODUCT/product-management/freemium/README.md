# VirtualDoc Freemium Tier (Community Edition)

## 🎯 Product Vision

The Freemium tier democratizes access to advanced healthcare technology by providing essential practice management tools for free, supported by community donations and optional premium features.

## 🏥 Target Users

### Primary Users
- **Individual Doctors**: Solo practitioners and independent doctors
- **Small Clinics**: 1-5 doctor practices
- **Developing Regions**: Healthcare providers in underserved areas
- **Students & Residents**: Medical students and residents
- **Non-profits**: Healthcare non-profit organizations

### User Personas

#### Dr. Sarah - Solo Family Physician
- **Location**: Rural town, 2,000 patients
- **Challenges**: Limited budget, needs basic practice management
- **Goals**: Digitize patient records, improve efficiency
- **Budget**: $0-50/month

#### Dr. Raj - Small Clinic Owner
- **Location**: Urban area, 3 doctors, 5,000 patients
- **Challenges**: Managing multiple doctors, basic reporting
- **Goals**: Streamline operations, improve patient care
- **Budget**: $100-300/month

#### Dr. Maria - Medical Student
- **Location**: University hospital
- **Challenges**: Learning healthcare technology
- **Goals**: Understand digital health tools
- **Budget**: $0/month

## 💰 Revenue Model

### Donation-Based Sustainability
- **Suggested Donation**: $5-50/month
