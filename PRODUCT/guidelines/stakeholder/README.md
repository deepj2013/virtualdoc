## 👥 Stakeholder Definition

### Who Are Stakeholders?
Stakeholders are individuals, groups, or organizations that have an interest in or are affected by the VirtualDoc platform project. They can influence or be influenced by the project's outcomes.

### Stakeholder Characteristics
- **Interest**: Level of interest in the project
- **Influence**: Ability to influence project decisions
- **Impact**: How the project affects them
- **Power**: Authority or control over project resources
- **Legitimacy**: Right to be involved in the project
- **Urgency**: Time sensitivity of their needs
