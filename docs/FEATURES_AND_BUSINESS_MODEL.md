## 💰 Business Model

### Freemium Model

#### Free Tier (Community Edition)
- **Cost**: $0/month
- **Target**: Individual practitioners, small clinics
- **Features**: Core healthcare management
- **Support**: Community support
- **Revenue**: Donations, premium upgrades

#### Premium Tier (Professional Edition)
- **Cost**: $50-200/month per provider
- **Target**: Medium practices, specialized clinics
- **Features**: AI features, advanced telemedicine
- **Support**: Priority support
- **Revenue**: Subscription fees

#### Enterprise Tier (Hospital Edition)
- **Cost**: $500-2000/month per organization
- **Target**: Large hospitals, healthcare systems
- **Features**: Full enterprise features
- **Support**: Dedicated support
- **Revenue**: Enterprise licensing

### Donation Model

#### Community Support
- **Individual Donations**: $5-100/month
- **Practice Donations**: $50-500/month
- **Corporate Sponsorships**: $1,000-10,000/month
